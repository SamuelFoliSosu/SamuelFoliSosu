## About Me 🚀

Samuel Foli Sosu is an IT Manager, Scanning Projects Coordinator, and [Data Protection Supervisor](https://cdpscertificates.dataprotection.org.gh/b52a5af0-ded4-4441-9867-9d1e9a4cf1ad#acc.DEBfTJsR) at [Top Archive](https://top-archive.com), a leading records and information management company in Ghana. He is also an artist at heart, passionate about creating, problem-solving, and building systems that leave a lasting impact.

With expertise in enterprise IT infrastructure and digital transformation, Samuel plays a pivotal role in maintaining network operations, overseeing system migrations, and optimizing document digitization workflows. As a Data Protection Supervisor, he ensures compliance with data protection regulations to safeguard sensitive information. His leadership in IT migration projects has facilitated seamless transitions for users across Ghana, Tanzania, Nigeria, and Israel, notably supporting the migration from GoDaddy to Microsoft 365 and domain integrations. Review his CV [here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1KgIh686LLTUdXW38XCto9pWB8YAZ_IuG/view).

Beyond his technical work, Samuel thrives in creative spaces. He has a deep love for arts, culture, and design, which he explores through graphic design. Music is also a core part of his life—he is currently learning to play both the piano and bass guitar, finding joy in the harmony of notes just as he does in coding efficient systems.

Samuel’s career is evolving toward full-stack development, driven by his love for problem-solving, logical thinking, and the power of technology to create real-world impact. He is currently on a 7-month journey to master back-end web development, focusing on server-side programming, databases, and API development.

Recognized for his dedication and excellence, he has received the Staff Appreciation Award (2023) and a Citation of Achievement (2020) for his leadership and contributions. 
Whether in tech, music, or design, Samuel’s mission is clear: to be a safe space, a source of courage, and the reason someone smiles.
